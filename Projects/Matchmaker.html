🃏 How to Play

  1. Flip one card onto the Left Pile.
  2. Flip one card onto the Right Pile.
  3. If the two cards have the same rank, you have made a Set.
  4. The two cards are placed into the Set pile.
  5. If they don't match, both cards go into the Discard Pile.
  6. Continue until the Draw Pile is empty.
  7. When the Draw Pile is empty, stack your Sets together to create a new Draw Pile.
  8. Keep going and see how many Sets you can make!

Suits don't matter — only the card's rank.
